Checkpoint 3 pre-repair workflow ownership reproduction
========================================================

The reproduction used the real FastAPI workflow routes with two projects and a
temporary SQLite database. Before the repair, identifiers owned by Project B
were accepted through Project A's route hierarchy.

Observed output:

project_b_version 200 sw_ver_08f0f3df45cf4a
project_a_gate_referencing_project_b_version 200 project_a sw_ver_08f0f3df45cf4a
version_with_missing_parent 200 missing_version
project_a_decides_project_b_gate 200 project_a sw_gate_23e584fb57b949
project_b_gate_status_after_cross_decision approved

Integrity failures demonstrated:

- An approval gate could point at another project's artifact version.
- A new version could point at a nonexistent source version.
- A decision submitted through Project A could approve Project B's gate.
- The cross-project decision changed canonical state instead of returning a
  deliberate ownership error.
