# Magic Demo Beats

## 1) One-Click Demo Flow
- What user sees: Click "Run Demo Flow" and land in a seeded cinematic project.
- Why it emotionally lands: Immediate momentum; no setup anxiety.
- Differentiator: Deterministic full-pipeline startup, not a fragile mock screen.

## 2) Cinematic Room Transitions
- What user sees: Coherent movement across Lobby → Study → Previs → Production → Screening → Crew Ops with preserved project context.
- Why it emotionally lands: Feels like one film journey, not disconnected tools.
- Differentiator: Intent continuity between rooms with shared project state.

## 3) Decision Gate
- What user sees: A readable readiness verdict and recommended next action.
- Why it emotionally lands: Clarity at decision moments; reduced ambiguity.
- Differentiator: Creative + operational gate, not just technical status.

## 4) Why This Shot Matters
- What user sees: A concise creative rationale line in Previs/Screening.
- Why it emotionally lands: The system explains significance, not just metadata.
- Differentiator: Human-readable cinematic intent grounding.

## 5) Creative Disagreement Mode
- What user sees: Occasional, subtle pushback when tradeoffs weaken emotional carryover.
- Why it emotionally lands: Feels like a thoughtful collaborator, not a compliance bot.
- Differentiator: Selective disagreement with uncertainty, no score theatrics.

## 6) Emerging Taste Read
- What user sees: Session-scoped preview of creative lean based on current revision emphasis.
- Why it emotionally lands: User feels seen without losing control.
- Differentiator: Preference preview without fake permanence or hidden profiling.

## 7) Production Pulse Board (Crew Ops)
- What user sees: Department readiness cards, pulse hero, and live ops notes tied to the same project.
- Why it emotionally lands: Confidence that the film can actually ship, not just generate.
- Differentiator: Final operational layer connected to creative context.
