# Do Not Become

## Product Soul Risks

### 1. Generic AI Agent Clutter
If every panel sounds like the same assistant, the product loses identity.

### 2. Autonomous Loop Chaos
Self-triggering workflows that users cannot reason about will destroy trust.

### 3. Fake Intelligence Metrics
Invented precision scores without grounded signals will be perceived as gimmickry.

### 4. Noisy Dashboards
If signal-to-noise collapses, creative users disengage quickly.

### 5. Over-Automation
Removing user agency from taste and story decisions makes the tool feel hostile.

### 6. Homogenized Storytelling
If all outputs converge to one "safe" aesthetic, the studio loses artistic value.

### 7. Feature Spam
A wide shallow surface beats depth only in roadmaps, not in demos.

### 8. Unreadable Analysis
Dense technical prose without creative meaning feels bureaucratic.

### 9. Infinite Workflow Complexity
If room transitions require explanation, the flow is too complex.

## What Would Make This Product Lose Its Soul
- Prioritizing architecture novelty over emotional cinematic payoff.
- Replacing creative collaboration with opaque automation.
- Treating storytelling as an afterthought to generation throughput.
- Shipping features that cannot produce a memorable creative moment.
