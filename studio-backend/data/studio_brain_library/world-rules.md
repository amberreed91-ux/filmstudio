# World Rules — Zombie Apocalypse

## The Infection
- **Vector:** Bite transmission only. Airborne exposure caused initial outbreak but is now inert — survivors have passive immunity to ambient exposure, not to direct fluid contact.
- **Turn time:** 4–18 hours depending on bite location and victim health. Closer to the brain = faster.
- **Pre-turn window:** Subject remains lucid but symptomatic — fever, confusion, emotional volatility. The most dangerous and dramatically rich phase.
- **There is no cure.** No subplot about a lab or a special immune person. That story is not this film.

## Zombie Behavior
- **Driven by sound and movement**, not smell or heat signature.
- **Slow individually, dangerous in mass.** A single zombie is manageable. Thirty zombies in a stairwell is death.
- **Retain residual muscle memory** in degraded form — they reach for door handles, cluster near old gathering spots (malls, churches, bus stops).
- **Decay timeline:** At current apocalypse stage (~14 months in), early-turned zombies are visibly deteriorating. Environmental attrition is working — harsh winters, dehydration, and physical damage are thinning numbers, slowly.
- **No running.** No intelligence. No communication between zombies. Horde behavior is purely acoustic.

## The World's Current State
- **14 months post-outbreak.** Initial chaos is over. Survivors in this story have made it through the worst and know it.
- **Government is gone** at federal level. Regional enclaves and warlord camps are the dominant power structures.
- **Infrastructure:** No grid power. Sporadic solar/generator pockets. No internet. Radio (AM) works in limited range — mostly silence and static, occasionally a looping government broadcast that may or may not still be current.
- **Supply reality:** First-wave looting stripped most urban stores. Survivors now farm, scavenge industrial/rural sites, and trade. Pharmaceuticals are gold. Fuel is running out.

## Rules the Characters Know
1. Never travel alone at night.
2. Sound discipline is survival. Raised voices get people killed.
3. A fresh bite is disclosed immediately. A concealed bite is a community death sentence.
4. You don't waste a bullet on a zombie you can walk around.

## What This World Is NOT
- Not a government conspiracy story.
- Not a cure-quest narrative.
- Not satirizing consumerism (that's been done; this film isn't interested).

## Studio Brain Application
Use world rules to ground all location, scene, and character advice. Flag any story beat that violates established infection logic or behavior rules. When a character decision seems irrational, check it against what they would realistically know at 14 months post-outbreak.
