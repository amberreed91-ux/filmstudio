# Judge Talking Points

## Creativity
- Room-based creative flow mirrors real film practice rather than one-shot generation.
- Co-director guidance is cinematic and contextual, not generic chatbot commentary.
- "Why this shot matters" turns data into intent-level storytelling feedback.

## Technical Depth
- End-to-end seeded pipeline verifies deterministic demo behavior across six rooms.
- Integrated route surfaces for assets, keyframes, analysis, embeddings, memory, review, package, and render operations.
- Frontend state coherence preserves `project_id` context through all room transitions.

## Product Thinking
- Each room answers a distinct user question: understand, shape, execute, review, operate.
- Progressive guidance reduces cognitive load while preserving user agency.
- Production pulse and decision gate unify creative and operational readiness.

## Emotional Intelligence
- Advisory language is cautious, human, and uncertainty-aware.
- Selective disagreement appears only in meaningful tradeoff contexts.
- Emerging Taste Read is session-scoped to avoid false permanence.

## Why This Is Not Just Another AI Wrapper
- Not prompt-in/prompt-out; it is workflow-native and memory-aware.
- Maintains narrative continuity and creative identity across revisions.
- Couples generation outputs with review, packaging, and operational decision layers.

## Why Memory/Context Matter
- Creative quality depends on recall of prior motifs, tone, and emotional pressure.
- Memory makes revisions comparable rather than isolated.
- Context keeps team decisions aligned from Study through Screening.

## Why Selective Disagreement Matters
- Constant agreement feels fake; constant critique feels hostile.
- Selective disagreement protects emotional intent without blocking workflows.
- It creates trust by surfacing tradeoffs in moments that actually matter.
